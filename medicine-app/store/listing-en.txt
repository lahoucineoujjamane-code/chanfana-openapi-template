App name (max 30 characters):
Herbal Doctor: Herbs & Health

Short description (max 80 characters):
Diseases, herbs and oils from world medicine, with an educational symptom guide

Full description:
🌿 Herbal Doctor is an offline health encyclopedia in three languages (Arabic, French, English).

📖 105 diseases: symptoms, causes, medical treatment, helpful herbs and oils, traditional remedies, lifestyle and when to see a doctor.

🌿 106 herbs and 22 oils: preparation, adult and child doses, pregnancy safety, contraindications and drug interactions, plus a section on toxic plants (harmal, oleander, pennyroyal…).

🌍 11 traditional medicine systems: Arabic & Prophetic, Moroccan & Amazigh, Greco-Islamic (Unani), Persian, Chinese, Ayurveda, Japanese Kampo, European, African and Native American.

🩺 Educational symptom guide: enter your age, sex and symptoms (165) to see the closest possibilities, with instant alerts for warning signs that need emergency care.

🧮 Child dose calculator for paracetamol and ibuprofen by weight.
💊 40 common medicines with doses and warnings.
⭐ Favorites, dark mode, search in any language.
🔒 Collects no personal data and works without internet.

⚕️ Disclaimer: for health education only. It does not provide a medical diagnosis and does not replace a doctor or pharmacist. In an emergency, call an ambulance.
