🌿 Privacy Policy

Herbal Doctor (طبيب الأعشاب / Médecin des Plantes) — package com.tabib.herbs — Last updated: 4 November 2026

English

  • No data collection. The app does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal or health information.
  • Works offline. All content is bundled inside the app. It makes no network requests, has no ads, no analytics and no third-party trackers.
  • Data on your device only. Your language, theme, favorites and the symptoms you enter in the symptom guide are saved only in the app's local storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.
  • No permissions are requested (no location, camera, contacts or storage access).
  • Children. The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
  • Medical disclaimer. The app is for health education only and does not provide a diagnosis or replace a doctor. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
  • Contact: Sabahshp@gmail.com
